OmniXRef: A Unified Framework for Multimodal Multi-Reference Video Editing

Xiaoyan Cong1* Tianyu Wang2 Hyeonho Jeong2 Zhifei Zhang2 Yuqian Zhou2 Yijun Li2 Shaoteng Liu2 Zhe Lin2 Srinath Sridhar1 Soo Ye Kim2†

1Brown University2Adobe Research

*Work done during an internship at Adobe Research†Corresponding author

Paper CodeSoon arXiv

TL;DRWe introduce OmniXRef, a unified framework for multimodal multi-reference video editing.
Our omni-reference set-up supports four dimensions: (i) number of references (1, ..., K), (ii) reference modality (text, image, video), (iii) reference attribute (camera, motion, content, style, VFX) and (iv) composition (text-image, text-video, image-video, text-image-video).
